Executive Take

AI 下一輪不是單純「更大的模型」，而是誰能把 AI 變成可運作的經濟基礎設施。

The next AI cycle is not only bigger models; it is about turning AI into operating economic infrastructure.

模型能力繼續上升，但真正的 buying decision 已經被 GPU availability、network fabric、power、cooling、security、governance、data locality 與 ROI 綁在一起。這使 HPE 的位置比純模型競賽更有利。

Model capability keeps rising, but buying decisions now bind compute to networking, power, cooling, security, governance, locality and ROI. That broadens HPE’s strategic surface area.
