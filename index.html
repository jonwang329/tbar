Executive Take

AI 下一輪不是單純「更大的模型」，而是誰能把 AI 變成可運作的經濟基礎設施。

The next AI cycle is not only bigger models; it is about turning AI into operating economic infrastructure.

模型能力繼續上升，但 buying decision 已被 GPU availability、network fabric、power、cooling、security、governance、data locality、utilization 與 ROI 綁在一起。這使 HPE 的位置比純模型競賽更有利。

Model capability keeps rising, but buying decisions now bind compute to networking, power, cooling, security, governance, locality, utilization and ROI. That broadens HPE’s strategic surface area.

政府與大型企業要的不是只有「自己的模型」，而是資料主權 + 運算主權 + operational control。Governments and enterprises need data sovereignty, compute sovereignty and operational control.
2 · Buying signal
NVIDIA ecosystem expansion、台灣 sovereign compute、能源／液冷投資共同支持 infrastructure expansion thesis。NVIDIA ecosystem expansion, Taiwan sovereign compute and power/cooling investment support the infrastructure thesis.
3 · Industry trend
AI 正從 model → agent → physical AI；每一步都增加 compute、network、security 與 operations complexity。AI is moving model → agent → physical AI; every step adds compute, networking, security and operational complexity.
